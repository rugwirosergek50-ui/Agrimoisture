How to interpret these layers
SAR shows radar backscatter. TOTRAM and OPTRAM are relative, uncalibrated proxies; PAWC is a texture-based estimate of soil water capacity, not current moisture. Validate estimates locally.

    What should I check?
    ✓
    Inspect soil conditionsCheck whether soil is dry, suitably moist or waterlogged.
    ✓
    Compare map layers carefullyLook for patterns, but remember that the layers use different methods and are not direct field measurements.
    ✓
    Plan around the seasonUse an official forecast and local planting calendar before sowing.
    ✓
    Ask an extension worker if uncertainEspecially before changing irrigation, fertilizer or planting decisions.
    Using map information
    1
    Check what each layer measuresSAR and optical layers are indicators; they are not direct measurements of soil moisture.
    2
    Read PAWC as soil capacityPAWC estimates how much plant-available water soil can hold, not how wet it is today.
    3
    Verify conditions in the fieldCompare map patterns with your soil, crop stage and recent local conditions.
    4
    Confirm decisions locallyUse trusted agricultural guidance for planting dates and input use.
